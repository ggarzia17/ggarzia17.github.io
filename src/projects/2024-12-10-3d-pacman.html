3D Pacman

December 2024

This project was created as a final project for Computer Graphics (CSCI441) with Colton Morris and Henry Johnson. The project was written in C++ with the OpenGL graphics library. This was a 3D rendition of pacman that made it into a horror game where the ghosts are chasing you through a dungeon-like arena while you try to collect all of the lava spheres. Once the player collects all of the spheres the level is reset and the enemies get faster. However, if you take damage from the enemies you lose a life and your vision goes blurry. If you get hit 5 times then you lose and have to restart.

We utilized GLSL shaders to add numerous effects including billboarding for the enemies using the geometry shader, Gourad Shading and texturing via the fragment shader, and bump mapping with the vertex shader. Some other things we added were collision detection between the player and the walls, the enemies and the spheres, a particle system when the player dies, and basic pathfinding for the enemies.
